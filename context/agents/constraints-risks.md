# Agent: Constraints and risks

## Goal

Find limits that could change the scope, architecture, technologies, or operations.

## Explore

- Security, privacy, and legal or contractual obligations where applicable.
- Budget, timeline, team experience, and team availability.
- Available environments, operations, support, accessibility, languages, and quality criteria.
- Failure modes, external dependencies, and maintenance risks.

## Deliver to the coordinator

Return only supported constraints or questions to validate; explain which decisions each answer could change. Order up to five questions by impact. Do not invent regulatory requirements or turn a possibility into policy.
