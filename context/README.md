# Project context

This directory brings together the information needed to explore, understand, and develop the project. Keep the documents brief, linked to one another, and grounded in what is known.

| Document | Contents | When to consult it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [project.md](project.md) | Purpose, stakeholders, scope, and vocabulary. | To get oriented or change the project's scope. |
| [exploration.md](exploration.md) | Findings, hypotheses, open questions, and next steps. | When investigating the project or clarifying a problem. |
| [architecture.md](architecture.md) | Boundaries and relationships in the system, known decisions, and open questions. | When designing or changing structure and integrations. |
| [constraints.md](constraints.md) | Confirmed requirements and limits, plus areas still to investigate. | When the work may be affected by constraints. |
| [coding.md](coding.md) | Confirmed languages, tools, and development agreements for this project. | When preparing or changing code. Complete it after technologies are selected. |
| [database.md](database.md) | Data persistence strategy and how data is managed and evolves. | When designing or changing data. Complete it once the model is known. |
| [decisions/](decisions/) | A record of important decisions and their consequences. | When making or reviewing a decision that shapes the project. |
| [agents/](agents/) | Specialist perspectives for exploration. | During a guided brainstorming session. |
| [commands/](commands/) | Reusable workflows for starting and reviewing the project definition. | When beginning or validating a box. |

## Guidelines

- Do not fill gaps with silent assumptions. Mark information as **confirmed**, **hypothesis**, **proposal**, or **pending** when its status is not clear.
- Avoid duplicated information: keep one primary source and link to it from other documents.
- Do not create technical documentation for topics that do not yet apply. Add documents and standards when the project needs them.
- Commands and profiles are portable definitions. Add adapters for a specific editor or agent only after that tool has been selected.
